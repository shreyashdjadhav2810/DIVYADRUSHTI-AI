In the digital world, cyber criminals may use fake links, misleading messages and false identities to deceive people. Awareness is the first step towards protection.

DEFENCE STRATEGY

How to Stay Protected

🛡️ Verify the website address before trusting it.

🛡️ Never share your OTP, password or UPI PIN.

🛡️ Avoid clicking unexpected links.

🛡️ Do not trust offers that seem too good to be true.

🛡️ Stop and verify before responding to urgent messages.
